Case studies

Client work

Three website rebuilds I built for clients while at Intrada Technologies: a law firm, a nonprofit, and a manufacturer. Each started with an outdated site and the same goal: make it modern, make it easy to manage, and make it work.

Law · WordPress

McCormick Law Firm

WordPressPHPCSSDNS
View live site ↗

The problem

The existing site had aged out. The design felt dated and didn't reflect the professionalism of the firm. The client needed a modern rebuild that would build trust on first contact with potential clients.

The work

A full WordPress rebuild from the ground up. I designed several pages from scratch with no existing template to follow (practice area pages, attorney profiles, and a contact section) while keeping the information architecture clean and the visual language consistent throughout.

The outcome

A clean, professional site built to instill confidence. Every design decision came from what a potential client would need to feel assured: clear credentials, accessible contact points, and a layout that gets out of its own way.

Nonprofit · WordPress

Families United Network

WordPressPHPCSSMigration
View live site ↗

The problem

The organization's site was built on Joomla, a platform the team found difficult to update and maintain without technical help. Content was going stale, and staff couldn't make basic changes on their own.

The work

A full migration from Joomla to WordPress, rebuilding the site from scratch in the process and designing multiple pages without an existing template. The priorities were a warm, accessible design suited to an adoption and foster care audience, and a backend that staff could actually use.

The outcome

A site the organization owns. Staff can update content, add resources, and manage pages without a developer. The design balances warmth and credibility, serving both families looking for support and donors evaluating the organization.

Manufacturing · WordPress

Lycoming Vault & Precast

WordPressPHPCSSForms
View live site ↗

The problem

The existing site wasn't doing the business justice. The client wanted a modern site that would actively sell their work, presenting products with the authority and clarity a trade audience expects.

The work

A full WordPress rebuild focused on product presentation: catalog pages, specifications, and a visual identity that reflects the weight and craft of precast concrete. Getting the contact forms to deliver inquiries reliably meant debugging form plugins and server-side mail configuration, then testing across environments.

The outcome

A site built for a trade audience that knows what it's looking for. Product information is easy to find, specifications are clearly presented, and the contact path works. The business now has a web presence that matches the quality of its work.
