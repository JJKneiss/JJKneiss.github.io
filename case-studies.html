Case Studies

Client Work

Three website rebuilds for real clients — a law firm, a nonprofit, and a manufacturer. Each started with an outdated site and a clear goal: make it modern, make it easy to manage, make it work.

WordPress · Law

McCormick Law Firm

mcclaw.com ↗
WordPress PHP CSS DNS
The Problem

The existing site had aged out — the design felt dated and didn't reflect the professionalism of the firm. The client needed a modern rebuild that would build trust on first contact with potential clients.

The Work

Full WordPress rebuild from the ground up. Designed several pages from scratch with no existing template to follow — practice area pages, attorney profiles, and a contact section — while keeping the information architecture clean and the visual language consistent throughout.

The Outcome

A clean, professional site built to instill confidence. Design decisions were driven by what a potential client would need to feel assured — clear credentials, accessible contact points, and a layout that gets out of its own way.

WordPress · Nonprofit

Families United Network

families4kids.org ↗
WordPress PHP CSS Migration
The Problem

The organization's site was built on Joomla — a platform the team found difficult to update and maintain without technical help. Content was going stale and staff couldn't make basic changes independently.

The Work

Full migration from Joomla to WordPress, rebuilding the site from scratch in the process. Designed multiple pages without an existing visual template to reference. The priority was a warm, accessible design that felt appropriate for an adoption and foster care audience — and a backend that staff could actually use.

The Outcome

A site the organization owns — staff can update content, add resources, and manage pages without a developer. The design balances warmth and credibility, serving both families seeking support and donors evaluating the organization.

WordPress · Manufacturing

Lycoming Vault & Precast

lycomingprecast.com ↗
WordPress PHP CSS Forms
The Problem

The existing site wasn't doing the business justice. The client wanted a modernized presence that would actively sell their work — showcasing products with the authority and clarity a trade audience expects.

The Work

Full WordPress rebuild with a focus on product presentation — catalog pages, specifications, and a visual identity that reflects the weight and craft of precast concrete work. A significant challenge was getting contact forms configured and reliably delivering inquiries, which required debugging form plugins, server-side mail configuration, and testing across environments.

The Outcome

A site built for a trade audience that knows what it's looking for — product information is easy to find, specifications are clearly presented, and the contact path works. The rebuild gave the business a web presence that matches the quality of the work they do.
