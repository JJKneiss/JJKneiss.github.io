Our history

How CHARTE Center began

The CHARTE Center was founded by therapists Callie Cooper and Howard Dobrushin in June of 2000. Howard and Callie envisioned an organization that would be unique while following all of their professional goals and ethical standards.

The inspiration for "professionalism with a human touch" came from a college professor's observation that psychotherapy competed with the hairdresser, bartender, and morning coffee klatch — informal spaces where people feel heard. That idea opened the door to an eclectic, client-first approach built on decades of combined clinical experience.

Our timeline

Key milestones

June 2000
CHARTE Center founded by Callie Cooper and Howard Dobrushin with a vision to provide client-centered therapeutic services.
April 2001
Doors officially open, absorbing contracts from Howard P. Dobrushin Consulting — including Life Projects, St. Francis Hospital, and Staunton Clinic.
Early growth
Wrap-Around services added in Allegheny County through Family Psychological Associates; agency contracted to CCBHO and Value Insurance.
Today
Continued focus on Behavioral Supports, Habilitation Aide services, and individual therapy for autistic and developmentally delayed populations, with 9 commercial insurance accreditations.
