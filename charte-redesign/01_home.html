What we offer

Client-centered care, every session

🧠
Cognitive behavioral therapy
Evidence-based individual therapy for anxiety, depression, mood disorders, and behavioral challenges across the lifespan.
👥
Family counseling
Collaborative therapy that strengthens family systems and supports caregivers navigating developmental or behavioral challenges.
💚
Autism & IDD support
Specialized services for individuals on the autism spectrum and those with intellectual and developmental disabilities.
📋
Bio-psychosocial assessments
Comprehensive intake evaluations informing individualized treatment plans aligned with CASSP principles and APA standards.
🤝
DBT & transactional analysis
Additional therapeutic modalities adapted to each client's unique goals, diagnosis, and personal circumstances.
📅
Flexible scheduling
Evening and daytime appointments available Monday through Friday, with in-person care in a welcoming environment.
