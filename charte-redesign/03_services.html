Core services

Three pillars of care

💙
Individual therapy
Our experienced staff works one-on-one with clients in the office to provide a therapeutic outlet, working together to foster growth, resilience, and lasting change. Available to children and adults of all ages and diagnoses.
🏡
Habilitation services
Hab Aide staff work closely with clients in the community to set meaningful goals and help them achieve greater independence and quality of life — one day at a time. Designed for individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities.
🛡
Behavioral supports
Behavioral Support Counselors work closely with autistic and developmentally disabled clients to build skills, reduce barriers, and empower them to thrive within their community and across life settings.
